# Market information

This context distinguishes provider-sourced market information from local
discovery labels and from facts established by the original disclosure.

## Language

**Source document**:
An original news article, research report, or statutory company announcement
identified by its own source and provenance. _Avoid_: verified event

**Discovery candidate**:
A source document selected by a bounded local query or classification rule.
Its label is a search aid, not a source-published fact. _Avoid_: confirmed event

**Disclosure event**:
A company action or reporting occurrence whose issuer, action, and relevant
details are supported by an original statutory disclosure. _Avoid_: headline match

**Source window**:
The explicit page, date range, or result cap actually inspected from one
provider. It does not imply complete historical or cross-provider coverage.
_Avoid_: full archive
